INTENDED USE
Intended Purpose: VascuStent Pro is intended for restoration of luminal patency in stenotic peripheral arteries in adult patients.
Evaluation Scope: Full clinical evaluation under MDR Annex XIV and MEDDEV 2.7/1 rev 4 including safety, performance, and benefit-risk profile.

LITERATURE AND EVIDENCE METHODS
Literature Search Strategy: Systematic search of PubMed, Embase, and Cochrane from 2014-2026 using terms including peripheral stent, restenosis, thrombosis, and vascular intervention outcomes.
Inclusion Criteria: Prospective studies, RCTs, and high-quality registries with >= 50 subjects and 6+ months follow-up.
Exclusion Criteria: Case reports, animal-only studies, studies with major protocol deviations, and cohorts under 10 subjects.
Clinical Data Sources: Published literature, manufacturer PMCF, complaint trending, vigilance reporting, and registry data.

BIOCOMPATIBILITY
Biocompatibility Standard: ISO 10993-1, ISO 10993-5, ISO 10993-10
Biological Endpoints Evaluated: Cytotoxicity, Sensitization, Irritation / Intracutaneous Reactivity
Cytotoxicity Result: Cytotoxicity ISO 10993-5: Pass (no cytotoxic response observed in L-929 assay).
Sensitization Result: Sensitization ISO 10993-10: Pass (no sensitization response observed).
Irritation Result: Irritation/Intracutaneous Reactivity ISO 10993-10: Pass (no irritation response observed).
Biocompatibility Assessment: All required biological endpoints passed and support clinical use for intended patient population.

BENEFIT-RISK AND CONCLUSION
Benefit-Risk Assessment: Clinical benefits in vessel patency and reduced reintervention outweigh residual risks when used per IFU.
Conclusions: Available evidence demonstrates acceptable safety and performance of VascuStent Pro for intended use.

Risk Management Standard: ISO 14971:2019

1. SCOPE
This report summarizes risk management activities for NeuroPulse Wearable Monitor intended for remote neurological signal monitoring.

2. HAZARD IDENTIFICATION
Hazard Identification: Skin irritation from prolonged patch contact; electrical over-stimulation due to firmware defect; data loss during Bluetooth disconnection.

3. RISK ESTIMATION
Risk Estimation: Skin irritation probability medium / severity low; over-stimulation probability low / severity high; data loss probability medium / severity medium.

4. RISK EVALUATION
Risk Evaluation: Over-stimulation risk is not acceptable without controls. Skin irritation and data loss require mitigations and post-market follow-up.

5. RISK CONTROL MEASURES
Risk Control Measures: Current limit firmware guardrails, automatic therapy shutoff, hypoallergenic adhesive selection, connectivity retry logic, and alerting for sync failures.

6. RESIDUAL RISK ASSESSMENT
Residual Risk Assessment: After controls, all identified risks are reduced to acceptable levels with documented rationale.

7. BENEFIT RISK RATIO
Benefit Risk Ratio: Clinical and operational benefits outweigh residual risks for intended use population.

8. BIOCOMPATIBILITY ASSESSMENT
Biocompatibility Standard: ISO 10993-1, ISO 10993-5, ISO 10993-10
Biological Endpoints Evaluated: Cytotoxicity, Sensitization, Irritation / Intracutaneous Reactivity
Cytotoxicity Result: Cytotoxicity ISO 10993-5: Pass (no cytotoxic response observed)
Sensitization Result: Sensitization ISO 10993-10: Pass (no sensitization response observed)
Irritation Result: Irritation/Intracutaneous Reactivity ISO 10993-10: Pass (no irritation response observed)
Biocompatibility Assessment: All required biological endpoints passed; no additional biocompatibility risk controls required.

9. CONCLUSION
The device risk profile is acceptable when operated within labeling constraints and implemented controls.
